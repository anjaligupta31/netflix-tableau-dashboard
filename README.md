# Netflix Content Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze Netflix's content library across movies and TV shows.

The dashboard provides insights into content growth over time, content-type distribution, genre composition, geographic availability, audience ratings, and individual title-level information.

The objective of the project is to transform Netflix content data into an interactive analytical dashboard that enables users to explore content trends and understand the composition of the Netflix catalog.

## Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows on Netflix
- Understand Netflix's content growth over the years
- Identify the most common content genres
- Analyze Netflix content distribution across countries
- Examine content ratings and audience classifications
- Explore individual titles through detailed information
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard for content analysis

## Key Analysis Areas

- Movies vs. TV Shows distribution
- Content additions by year
- Top genres
- Geographic distribution of content
- Content ratings
- Release year
- Date added to Netflix
- Title-level content details
- Genre and content-type filtering

## Key Insights

### 1. Movies dominate the Netflix content library

Movies account for approximately 68.4% of the titles in the dataset, with 4,265 movies compared with 1,969 TV shows.

This indicates that movies form the majority of Netflix's content catalog in the analyzed dataset.

### 2. Netflix has a significantly larger movie catalog

The dataset contains a total of 6,234 movies and TV shows, with movies representing the larger share of the content library.

The movie-to-TV-show ratio highlights the strong emphasis on movie content within the analyzed Netflix catalog.

### 3. Content additions increased substantially in later years

The year-wise visualization shows a strong increase in the number of movies and TV shows added to the platform over time, particularly during the later years of the dataset.

This reflects the rapid expansion of Netflix's content catalog during the period analyzed.

### 4. Netflix content spans a broad range of genres

The dashboard's Top 10 Genre visualization highlights major content categories including Documentaries, Stand-Up Comedy, Dramas, International Movies, Independent Movies, Comedies, Kids' TV and Children & Family Movies.

This demonstrates the diversity of content available across different audience segments.

### 5. Mature content ratings represent a significant portion of the catalog

TV-MA and TV-14 are among the most prominent ratings in the dataset, with TV-MA representing the largest rating category.

This indicates a substantial presence of mature and teen-oriented content in the Netflix catalog.

### 6. Content is geographically diverse

The country-level visualization shows that Netflix content is distributed across a wide range of countries, highlighting the international nature of the platform's content library.

### 7. Individual title analysis provides additional context

The dashboard allows users to select a title and examine attributes such as description, date added, duration, rating, release year, genre and content type.

For example, the selected title "2,215" is a documentary released in 2018, added to Netflix in 2019, with a duration of 89 minutes and a TV-14 rating.


## Analytical Takeaways

- Netflix's catalog is heavily weighted toward movies compared with TV shows.
- The substantial increase in content additions over time indicates rapid catalog expansion.
- The diversity of genres suggests that Netflix targets multiple audience segments rather than relying on a single content category.
- Rating analysis provides an additional perspective for understanding the maturity and target audience of the catalog.
- Geographic analysis can be used to understand Netflix's international content footprint.
- Combining content type, genre, country, rating and release year provides a multidimensional view of Netflix's content strategy.
